This is a fan-hosted system reference document for Stonetop, and not officially affiliated with the writers or publishers of that game.

Stonetop is a “hearth fantasy” tabletop RPG set in an Iron Age that never was. The players portray the heroes of an isolated village near the edge of the known world. Their adventures focus on dealing with threats to the village and seizing opportunities. These aren’t rootless mercenaries seeking fortune and glory; they’re exceptional people, taking risks on behalf of their friends, family, and neighbors. The game was written by Jeremy Strandberg.
